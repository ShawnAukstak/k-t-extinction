K–T Extinction Simulator

Watch the Chicxulub asteroid strike the Earth 66 million years ago and follow what came next: the fireball, the ejecta curtain, the tsunami, and the years of darkness and cold that ended the age of the dinosaurs. It plays out on a reconstructed Late Cretaceous globe with the plants and animals of the time, and every event links to the research it is based on.
